## Notes on Data Quality

### Limitations and Caveats

1. **Transparency Gap**: Major AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) do not publicly disclose detailed energy consumption data
2. **Market vs Location-Based Accounting**: Significant discrepancies exist between reported emissions (market-based with RECs) and actual grid emissions (location-based)
3. **Inference Estimates**: Per-query energy estimates vary widely (0.3-20+ Wh) depending on model size, query complexity, and infrastructure
4. **Projection Uncertainty**: 2030 projections have wide ranges due to uncertainty in AI adoption rates, efficiency improvements, and energy mix changes
5. **Embodied Carbon**: Most estimates focus on operational emissions; embodied carbon from hardware manufacturing adds 20-50% to lifecycle emissions

- IEA "Energy and AI" report: Published April 2025
- Company sustainability reports: FY2024 data (published May 2025)
- Goldman Sachs projections: Updated December 2025
